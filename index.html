Frequently Asked Questions

Will pressure washing damage my concrete pavers or exterior paint? +

Never. We use pressure-rated surface rotary cleaners rather than dangerous needle jets for flat surfaces, which evenly cleans without gouging cement. For stucco, eaves, and painted surfaces, we strictly employ low-pressure Soft Washing utilizing botanical and mild cleaners.

Are your cleaning chemicals safe for tropical gardens, pets, and children? +

Yes, absolutely. As proud plant enthusiasts ourselves, we pre-soak, bag sensitive flora when required, and continuously rinse garden beds with fresh clean water throughout the washing process. Our biodegradable solutions neutralize harmlessly in the soil.

Which locations across Cavite and nearby do you service? +

We provide full service to all subdivisions and private estates in Silang, Tagaytay, Dasmariñas, Imus, Bacoor, General Trias, and Santa Rosa (Nuvali / Laguna border).
