## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an immediate sense of reliability, operational precision, and commercial authority for exterior cleaning and pressure washing services. Tailored for homeowners, property managers, and commercial clients in Cavite and adjacent regions, the tone balances industrial competence with domestic respect. It conveys the crisp freshness of pristine restoration, removing skepticism around service reliability, damage risks, and hidden costs.

The design movement combines **Modern Corporate** with **High-Utility Direct Response**:
- **Pristine Precision**: Clean white and cool slate grounds reflect physical cleanliness and meticulous attention to detail.
- **Authoritative Grounding**: Deep maritime navy establishes architectural stability and vetted legitimacy.
- **Hydraulic Freshness**: Sky and cyan dynamic tones invoke water flow, modern cleaning equipment, and gleaming results.
- **Conversion Urgency**: High-contrast, industrial safety-amber highlights conversion funnels, pricing toggles, and direct-response triggers without devolving into visual spam.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for high trust, stark optical clarity, and decisive conversion architecture.

### Primary Anchor (#0F2B48)
Used for primary brand architecture, dark navigation bars, authoritative headlines, structural containers, and footer baselines. It gives the weight of an established, fully insured contractor.

### Secondary Water Accent (#0284C7)
Represents pressurized water streams, spotlessness, and modern sanitation technology. Applied to badges, highlighted metrics, icon containers, tabs, and interactive hover outlines. A subtle secondary range extends to `#0EA5E9` for gradient fills on badges and active status pips.

### Tertiary Action Amber/Orange (#EA580C)
Reserved exclusively for high-intent conversion paths: phone call CTA buttons, instant-quote triggers, sticky conversion banners, and promotional claim buttons. It commands an immediate visual hierarchy against navy and slate surfaces.

### Neutral System & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) ensures optimal reading contrast and a sanitized aesthetic.
- **Surface Dim / Warm Stone**: Slate 50 (`#F8FAFC`) and Slate 100 (`#F1F5F9`) for alternate section backing, before-and-after review cards, and quote calculators.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Slate 200 (`#E2E8F0`) providing tactile, clean separation without heavy visual noise.
- **Body Typography**: Slate 700 (`#334155`) for balanced readability without the harsh glare of pure black.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric authority and human touch of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and call-to-actions with the utilitarian, screen-optimized readability of **Inter** for descriptions, guarantee clauses, and lead capture inputs.

- Headings use tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic architectural signage and create punchy, high-impact statements.
- Direct-response elements—such as pricing figures, turnaround timers, and service badges—leverage `label-md` and `label-sm` in bold uppercase treatments with slight positive tracking to ensure fast scanning during rapid scrolling.
- Body text retains an open 1.625 line-height ratio (`lineHeight: 26px` on `16px` font) to guarantee effortless readability in outdoor or high-glare environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements an 8pt architectural rhythm structured around a 12-column responsive fluid grid with a strictly enforced maximum width of `1200px` for optimal scanning length.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with 24px (`space-lg`) gutters and fluid container padding. Allows 7:5 split between sales copy and live estimation funnel forms.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with 20px gutters. Hero sections stack symmetrically; multi-step estimation flows shift to full-width containers.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4 columns with 16px (`space-md`) gutters and 16px outer margins. Form inputs and direct-call ribbons lock full-width to preserve finger-tap accuracy.

### Direct Response Vertical Cadence
- **Section Spacing**: High-contrast service transitions use `space-4xl` (96px) on desktop and `space-2xl` (48px) on mobile to maintain visual energy without clutter.
- **Stacking Groupings**: Card-internal groupings adhere to `space-xs` (8px) between icon and header, `space-sm` (12px) between header and text, and `space-lg` (24px) before CTAs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined physical model: ultra-clean structural surfaces anchored by low-contrast architectural borders, supported by crisp directional ambient drop shadows. Surfaces feel elevated, durable, and tactile.

### Shadow Structure
- **Level 1 (Feature Cards, Review Tiles)**: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 43, 72, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 43, 72, 0.05)`. Encased within a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Level 2 (Active Quotes, Conversion Containers, Hover Cards)**: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 43, 72, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 43, 72, 0.04)`. Brings the conversion form physically forward from the canvas.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Conversion Bar, Modal Dialogues, Floating Call Triggers)**: `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(15, 43, 72, 0.16), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 43, 72, 0.08)`.
- **CTA Button Glow (High-Intent Action Focus)**: `0 4px 14px 0 rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.38)`. Provides depth directly below the primary conversion button, indicating immediate physical pressability.

### Structural Depth
Transparent overlays (`rgba(15, 43, 72, 0.75)`) backed with a `blur(12px)` filter are utilized exclusively on hero image backgrounds to ensure text contrast passes WCAG AAA standards across all daylight conditions.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometry. Radii are crisp and controlled, communicating engineering discipline, safety, and modern craftsmanship rather than playful consumer frivolity.

- **Base Corner Radius (`rounded-sm`)**: 4px. Used on mini badges, status tags, micro-badges, and form radio indicators.
- **Component Corner Radius (`rounded-md`)**: 8px. Used on primary conversion buttons, input text fields, select dropdowns, and before-and-after interactive slider handles.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`)**: 12px. Used on pricing cards, quote review panels, service package containers, and lead generation form wrappers.
- **Full Radius (`rounded-full`)**: Applied strictly to verification checkmarks, telephone floating icons, and rating count pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Instant Quote / Call Now)**: Background in tertiary orange (`#EA580C`) with hover state `#C2410C`. Text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), `label-lg` typography, 14px 28px padding, 8px border radius, accompanied by an ambient orange drop shadow. Supports an optional right-aligned directional arrow icon.
- **Secondary Authority (View Guarantee / See Results)**: Navy solid (`#0F2B48`) or 1.5px outline in `#0F2B48` with transparent interior. Text in `#0F2B48`, shifting to pure white with solid fill on hover.
- **Click-to-Call Sticky Anchor**: Full-width mobile fixed bar, high-contrast orange background with large phone icon and explicit microcopy: *"Tap to Call Silang Headquarters — Instant Response"*.

### Trust Badges & Verification Chips
- Compact pills utilizing an icon-left layout. Background is Slate 100 (`#F1F5F9`) with a 1px border of `#CBD5E1`.
- Featured with a `#0284C7` checkmark icon or 5-star cluster in `#F59E0B`. 
- Typography: `label-sm` in `#0F2B48`. Examples: *"Silang & Cavite Certified"*, *"Fully Insured & Bonded"*, *"Eco-Safe Detergents"*.

### Quote Calculator & Direct-Response Forms
- **Input Fields**: 48px height minimum to ensure mobile tap targets. Background is `#FFFFFF`, border is 1.5px `#CBD5E1`, border-radius 8px. Active focus state transitions to a 2px `#0284C7` border with a 3px outer ring tinted `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.
- **Field Labels**: `label-md` in `#0F2B48`, paired with lightweight inline helper text in `#64748B`.
- **Step Selectors (Property Type / Surface Cleaning)**: Large selectable square cards with 12px padding, subtle 1px slate borders, interactive checked state showing a `#0284C7` outer border and faint cyan tint (`#F0F9FF`).

### Before-and-After Comparison Card
- Dual-image split canvas with an interactive high-contrast white draggable dividing bar.
- Overlay badges positioned at opposite bottom corners: *"Before: Heavy Algae & Grime"* (dark charcoal backer) vs. *"After: ClearView Pressure Restored"* (cyan/blue backer).

### Cards & Service Packages
- Clean white surfaces with 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `Level 1` shadow. 
- Header features an icon container tinted with 10% `#0284C7` fill and a solid blue glyph. 
- Includes bulleted checkmark lists highlighting specific service inclusions, ending with a localized price or transparent instant estimate trigger.
